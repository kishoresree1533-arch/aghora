
# AGHORA MEP Engineering Consultants Website

## Overview
A modern, professional 6-page corporate website for AGHORA MEP Engineering Consultants, showcasing your expertise in HVAC, Electrical, Plumbing, Fire Protection & ELV systems. The design will be clean, engineering-focused, and premium with a color palette inspired by your logo (dark navy blue, sky blue accents, white, and grey).

---

## Design System
- **Primary Colors**: Dark navy blue (#1a365d) and sky blue (#0ea5e9)
- **Neutral Colors**: White, light grey, charcoal grey
- **Typography**: Modern sans-serif fonts for a clean, professional look
- **Style**: Subtle animations, professional icons (Lucide), and generous whitespace

---

## Pages

### 1. Home Page
- **Hero Section**: Full-width banner with tagline "Where Innovation is Engineered", CTAs for "Contact Us" and "Request Consultation"
- **Services Overview**: 5 service cards (HVAC, Electrical, Plumbing, Fire Protection, ELV)
- **Why Choose Us**: Key differentiators with icons
- **Statistics Section**: Animated counters (Years of Experience, Projects Completed, Happy Clients, Team Members)
- **Client Testimonials**: Rotating testimonial carousel
- **CTA Banner**: "Ready to start your project?"

### 2. About Us Page
- **Company Introduction**: Mission, vision, and company story
- **Our Values**: Core values with visual icons
- **Leadership Team**: Team member cards with photos and bios
- **Certifications & Affiliations**: Display of credentials

### 3. Services Page
- **Service Categories**: Detailed sections for each discipline:
  - HVAC Systems Design
  - Electrical Engineering
  - Plumbing & Drainage
  - Fire Protection Systems
  - ELV Systems (Security, AV, Data)
- Each service includes description, capabilities, and relevant icons

### 4. Training Page
- **Training Programs**: Available courses and certifications
- **Training Benefits**: Why choose AGHORA for training
- **Course Catalog**: Cards for different training modules
- **Registration CTA**: "Enroll Now" buttons

### 5. Projects Page
- **Project Portfolio**: Grid of completed projects with images
- **Filter by Category**: Sort by service type
- **Project Cards**: Each showing project name, type, scope, and location
- **Featured Projects**: Highlighted case studies

### 6. Contact Page
- **Contact Form**: Name, email, phone, service interest, message
- **Office Information**: Address, phone, email
- **Interactive Map Placeholder**: Location display
- **Business Hours**: Operating schedule

---

## Shared Components
- **Navigation**: Sticky header with logo, navigation links, and "Request Consultation" CTA button
- **Footer**: Company info, quick links, services, contact details, social media icons
- **Mobile Menu**: Hamburger menu with smooth slide-in animation
